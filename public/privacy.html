Analytics privacy

Optional Google Analytics measures visits, approximate location, device information, engagement and interactions such as project links and PDF download clicks. It uses cookies after you accept. Advertising personalization is disabled. Analytics is not intended to collect contact form contents.

You can decline analytics or change your choice using Analytics privacy on the portfolio. Your preference is stored in your browser. Declining does not affect access to the portfolio.

See Google's privacy policy for information about its processing.
